These sound effects, the original concert melody, and their recordings were
created specifically for this project on 2026-11-20. They use synthesized sine
waves, envelopes, and original note sequences. No external recordings, sampled
instruments, existing songs, or third-party audio assets were used.

Source and production
Original project-generated synthesis, 24 kHz mono PCM encoded with FFmpeg
libopus into WebM. Short effects use 32 kbit/s Opus. The concert track uses
24 kbit/s Opus and a twenty-second loop of plucked pentatonic tones with soft
bass pulses. This license applies to the audio assets listed above.
